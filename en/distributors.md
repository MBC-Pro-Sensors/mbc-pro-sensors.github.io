# 🤝 Become a Distributor

We are looking for distributors who serve robotics teams, schools and training centers in their country. If your customers use LEGO® Education SPIKE™ Prime, MINDSTORMS® EV3 or Arduino for competitions such as WRO, MBC-Pro Sensors is a natural add-on to your range.

## Who We Are Looking For

- Robotics and STEM education suppliers
- LEGO® Education resellers
- Robotics training centers and competition organizers' partners
- Maker and electronics retailers serving schools

## Why Carry MBC-Pro Sensors

- **Solves real competition problems:** 8/16-channel line followers, 180° laser ranging, a 6-port expander for SPIKE™ Prime, PS4/PS5 gamepad receivers and a vision camera. See [all products](/en/README.md#products).
- **Plug and play:** connects directly to SPIKE™ Prime and EV3 ports and works with the official apps and Pybricks. No adapter boards.
- **Ready for WRO:** a clear [WRO Rules Check](/en/wro-rules.md) for every product, so your customers know what they can use in each age group.
- **Full English documentation:** product pages, setup guides, a [Buying Guide](/en/guide.md), an [FAQ](/en/faq.md) and a shared [Universal I2C Protocol](/en/i2c-protocol.md).
