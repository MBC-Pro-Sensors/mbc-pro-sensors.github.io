# 🔒 Privacy Policy

This website (mbc-pro-sensors.github.io, "the Site") is operated by Shaobo Creative Robotics Company Limited (MBC Robot). We respect your privacy. This page explains what data the Site collects and how it is used.

## What We Collect

The Site **requires no registration** and never asks for your name, phone number or other personal details.

To understand how the Site is used and improve its content, the Site uses **Google Analytics** to collect anonymous browsing data, such as:

- Pages viewed and time spent
- How you arrived (e.g. a search engine or social network)
- Device type, browser and approximate location (country / city level)
- Buttons clicked (e.g. "Ask on LINE", file downloads)

This data is aggregated statistically; we **cannot** identify you from it.

## Cookies

Google Analytics uses cookies in your browser to tell visitors apart. You can:

- Block or delete cookies in your browser settings
- Install the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)

For how Google uses this data, see [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://www.google.com/policies/privacy/partners/).

## Information You Give Us

When you contact us via our LINE official account or email, we use what you provide only to answer your questions, quote and ship orders. We do not share it with unrelated third parties.

## Our Facebook Page and the Meta API

We manage our own Facebook Page through Meta's official API (app "MBC 粉專管理"), only to:

- Publish, edit and schedule posts on our Page
- Read, reply to and moderate comments left on our Page
- Read engagement data for our Page's posts to measure reach

In doing so we may see the display name and content of comments you post publicly on our Page. We use them only to reply to and moderate comments. We **do not** build marketing lists from them, sell them or share them with third parties, and we do not access your private messages or personal account data. We keep no separate database of followers; the access token used to manage the Page is stored only on devices we control.

## Your Rights and Data Deletion

Under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act you may ask us at any time to review, copy, correct, stop processing or delete your personal data.

To have data we handled for the purposes above deleted (for example, to have your comment on our Page removed or hidden), email [contactmbcrobot@gmail.com](mailto:contactmbcrobot@gmail.com) with your Facebook display name and a link to the post. We will act on it and reply **within 30 days**. You can also delete your own comments on our Page at any time.
