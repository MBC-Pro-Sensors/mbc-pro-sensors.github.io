# IMU 9-Axis Gyro × BBC micro:bit V1 / V2

Using the IMU 9-Axis Gyro with BBC micro:bit V1 / V2: check the edition and the wiring, then pick your software; its page covers that software only.

<figure class="guide-hero"><img src="/images/illust/host-microbit.webp" alt="BBC micro:bit V1 / V2"></figure>

## Which edition

Get the **Universal I2C edition** and connect it to the I2C header of a micro:bit breakout (SDA = P20, SCL = P19), factory address `0x6A`. The sensor needs 5 V; use a breakout board with a 5 V output.
