# Pathfinder 8-Way (Line8) × MATRIX Mini R4

Using the Pathfinder 8-Way (Line8) with MATRIX Mini R4: check the edition and the wiring, then pick your software; its page covers that software only.

<figure class="guide-hero"><img src="/images/illust/host-matrix.webp" alt="MATRIX Mini R4"></figure>

## Which edition

Get the **MATRIX edition** and connect it with the stock MATRIX 4-pin cable to I2C1 or I2C2 of the Mini R4 (address `0x22`); pick that port in the block.
