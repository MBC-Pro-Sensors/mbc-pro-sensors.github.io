# Ranger 8-Way (TOF8) × SPIKE Prime / Robot Inventor

Using the Ranger 8-Way (TOF8) with SPIKE Prime / Robot Inventor: check the edition and the wiring, then pick your software; its page covers that software only.

<figure class="guide-hero"><img src="/images/illust/host-spike.webp" alt="SPIKE Prime / Robot Inventor"></figure>

## Which edition

Get the **SPIKE edition** (LEGO LPF2 plug) and plug it into any hub port A~F; no battery needed. Works with SPIKE Prime (yellow hub) and Robot Inventor (teal hub).
